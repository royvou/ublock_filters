! Reserved listings: match only the price label. One substring prefilter covers the
! legacy, structured and hashed CSS-module price classes; the exact text check
! rejects other price labels.
marktplaats.nl##:is(.hz-Listing,.hz-StructuredListing) [class*="Listing-price"]:has-text(/^\s*Gereserveerd\s*$/i):upward(.hz-Listing,.hz-StructuredListing)

! Ordinary related recommendations can be useful; these are opt-in.
marktplaats.nl###similar-items-root,.SimilarAdsList-related-ads-section

! "Ook van deze adverteerder": hide the recommendation strip, keep its main listing.
marktplaats.nl##[data-testid="listing-other-seller"]
